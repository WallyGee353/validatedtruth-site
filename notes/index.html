Author’s Notes

You marked your last checkbox — and the questions didn’t stop. These notes continue the same discipline beyond the book’s 155 claims: a passage in full context, a brief observation, and a question you answer yourself.

The book never asks what you concluded — your tally is yours. But many readers finish holding one question the book deliberately leaves open: if the claims did not confirm, then who is the Savior? You are not the first to ask it. The Creator answered that question Himself, in His own voice — and these notes simply open those passages, in full context, so you can read His answer for yourself.

One honest label before you read: the book takes no position — these notes are different. Here, Wally Gee writes as a reader with conclusions of his own, showing the scriptures that led him there. The method is the same; the voice is the author’s. Verify everything for yourself.
