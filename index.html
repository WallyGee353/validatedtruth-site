The first book

The Bible — One Book, Two Voices

This book presents New Testament claims alongside the Old Testament passages they reference, each one printed in full context, nothing added, nothing removed. At the end of each passage, the reader is asked a question about what the passage says.

Minimal commentary. No interpretation. No answers provided. The text speaks for itself. The decision is yours.

155 claims, in full context

Each New Testament claim is set beside the Old Testament passage it references — quoted from the public-domain World English Bible, exactly as it appears in Scripture.

Simple questions, three choices

Who is speaking? Who is the subject? Does the passage identify the subject the same way? You mark Yes, Maybe, or No. There are no right answers except your own.

Your own tally

887 questions. 3,126 checkboxes. By the last page, the verdict on every claim is in your handwriting — not anyone else’s.

This book is not a replacement for your Bible. It is a companion to it. You are strongly encouraged to follow along in your own copy — and to verify everything for yourself.
