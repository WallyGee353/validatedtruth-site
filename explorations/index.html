Everything in this section is an exploration — a pattern that lines up between confirmed scientific findings and the text of Scripture, set side by side so you can look at it yourself. These are offered as observations and open questions, not as proof and not as confirmed fact.

“Assumption Is Not Confirmation” applies here first, to my own work. A parallel that is striking is still only a parallel. Where a finding is settled science, it is stated as such; where the connection to Scripture is my own — a “what if this is pointing at something” — that is exactly what it is, and nothing more. Read the two columns, and decide for yourself what, if anything, they mean.
