The Genesis series

A multi-volume study of Genesis, written the same way as The Bible — One Book, Two Voices: the passage in full context, a short observation, and a question you answer yourself.

The first book is finished and for sale. This series is not. There is no volume list here, because the titles and the order have not been supplied for this page.

What has been said, in the author’s own note, is this:

“I stopped writing it to build the tools to verify every claim in it before you ever read one. Assumption Is Not Confirmation applies to my own work first. When it is ready, this is where you will hear about it.”

Wally Gee, Author’s Note 002 — The Waters Above

That note is the sample that is already public: Genesis 1:1–8, Day Two, the raqia, and the waters above. The note at the end of it is marked as the author’s. The book takes no position. The notes do, and they say so.
